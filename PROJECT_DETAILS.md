# PrepGen AI - Comprehensive Project Reference

This document serves as the foundational reference material for creating the **Lean Canvas**, **Concept Note**, and **Pitch Deck (PPT)** for PrepGen AI. It synthesizes the problem, solution, technical architecture, and business model into a structured format.

---

## 1. Project Overview
- **Name:** PrepGen AI
- **Tagline:** "Your Personal AI-Powered Study Workspace and Document Companion."
- **One-Sentence Pitch:** PrepGen AI is an intelligent workspace that instantly transforms any topic or document into structured, interactive study materials, complete with an AI assistant that explains complex concepts like you're five.

## 2. The Problem (For Lean Canvas & Concept Note)
Students, researchers, and professionals face significant friction when trying to synthesize and understand new information:
1. **Information Overload:** Reading lengthy texts or researching new topics takes too much time and often results in poor retention.
2. **Context Switching:** Users have to juggle multiple tabs—reading a document in one tab, asking ChatGPT questions in another, and taking notes in a third.
3. **Passive Learning:** Most AI summarization tools just spit out text, leading to passive reading rather than active engagement and memorization.
4. **Complex Jargon:** High-level academic papers and technical documents are notoriously difficult for beginners to parse.

## 3. The Solution (For Lean Canvas & Concept Note)
PrepGen AI provides a **unified, intelligent workspace** that bridges reading, learning, and interacting.
1. **Instant Note Generation:** Users simply type a topic, and PrepGen generates comprehensive, beautifully formatted markdown study notes.
2. **Integrated Workspace Assistant:** A persistent AI chat panel sits right next to the document, allowing users to ask contextual questions, generate summaries, or rewrite sections without ever leaving the page.
3. **ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5):** A dedicated feature that instantly breaks down highlighted text or complex concepts into simple, universally understandable analogies.
4. **Active Recall (SRS):** Built-in flashcard generation based on the notes for long-term retention.

## 4. Target Audience / Customer Segments
- **High School & University Students:** Looking to compress study time, prepare for exams, and simplify complex coursework.
- **Researchers & Academics:** Needing to quickly distill large volumes of text into digestible outlines.
- **Lifelong Learners & Professionals:** Upskilling and learning new domains rapidly (e.g., tech, finance, law).

## 5. Key Value Proposition (Unique Selling Proposition)
**"Contextual AI, not just Conversational AI."** 
Unlike standard ChatGPT interfaces, PrepGen AI grounds its intelligence in the user's specific document. It combines the power of a modern text editor (like Notion) with a tailored AI tutor, removing the friction of context-switching and maximizing learning efficiency.

## 6. Core Features Breakdown (For Pitch Deck)

### A. The Document Viewer & Outliner
- **Dynamic Table of Contents:** Automatically parses document headings to create a beautiful, slide-over navigation tree for instant document traversal.
- **Rich Markdown Rendering:** Code highlighting, structured lists, and clean typography for optimal readability.

### B. The Workspace Assistant (AI Tutor)
- **Persistent AI Chat:** A contextual sidebar (responsive on both mobile and desktop) that acts as a personal tutor for the active document.
- **Context-Aware:** The AI knows exactly what document you are looking at and tailors its answers accordingly.

### C. Community & Library
- **Community Notes:** A shared repository where users can browse, read, and clone high-quality notes generated by others directly into their personal workspace.
- **Local-First Storage:** User history is securely stored on their device, ensuring privacy and fast access.

### D. The ELI5 Engine
- Specifically tuned AI endpoints designed to strip away jargon and explain things using analogies.

## 7. Technical Architecture & Stack
*This highlights the platform's modern, scalable, and cost-effective design.*

- **Frontend Framework:** React + Vite (for lightning-fast builds and modular UI).
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (for highly responsive, premium glassmorphism aesthetics).
- **Animations:** Framer Motion (for smooth, application-like transitions and slide-over panels).
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite` for high-throughput, low-latency contextual generation).
- **Storage:** IndexedDB & LocalStorage (Local-first architecture for zero-latency history retrieval).
- **Deployment:** Vercel (Edge-optimized global delivery).

## 8. Business Model & Monetization (For Lean Canvas)
PrepGen AI operates on a **Freemium** model based on a usage credit system.

- **Free Tier:** Users get a set number of "Credits" per day. Generating notes or asking the assistant costs credits. 
- **Pro Tier (Subscription):** ~$8-10/month. Unlocks unlimited Gemini API calls, longer document generation, advanced PDF OCR parsing, and cross-device cloud sync.
- **Pay-as-you-go:** Users can top up credits if they just need a temporary boost for exam week.

---

## 9. Unfair Advantage / Moat
- **UI/UX Excellence:** An uncompromising focus on a premium, fluid user experience (animations, mobile responsiveness) that feels like a native app.
- **High Efficiency Prompts:** Proprietary prompt engineering on the backend that ensures note generation is highly structured and educational, not just generic text.

## 10. Future Roadmap (For PPT Vision Slide)
1. **PDF & OCR Uploads:** Allow users to upload scanned PDFs, with the AI reading and explaining the text client-side.
2. **Cloud Sync:** Transition from local storage to a database (e.g., Supabase) to allow users to access their workspace seamlessly across phone, tablet, and laptop.
3. **Collaborative Multiplayer:** Allow multiple students to inhabit the same workspace and chat with the same AI tutor simultaneously.
